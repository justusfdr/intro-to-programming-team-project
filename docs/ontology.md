A web app that brings calendar, goals and focused work together, and blocks distracting websites in Chrome.
Rule for coding agents: read this file first. Do not change it without the team's approval; propose the change and wait.

## Purpose and scope
- **Problem and user.** Anyone who wants to be more productive and is constantly distracted while planning across three or four tools: students, employees, self-employed people, in private life or at work. Example persona: a student in the exam phase (lectures, a statistics exam, half-marathon training).
- **User journey.** Sign up, set goals with weekly rules (a new goal through six short questions (added 16 Nov 2026, spec 42: decided in the team meeting of 16 Nov 2026; the team confirms with the merge)), note tasks, plan days, focus while sites are blocked, mark events done, see progress.
- **One role:** the signed-in user. No admin. Nothing is shared between users, except that contacts see each other's display name, photo, e-mail address, bio, LinkedIn link and time zone (R43, R52 to R54), and what a user chooses to share with one of their contacts: their calendar, free/busy or with details, read only (R55 to R58) (added 18 Nov 2026, spec 53, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge).
- **Two plans:** Free has everything except the AI chat; Pro adds it, paid through Stripe in test mode.

## 2 Relationships
- Every saved row belongs to one user, who has one plan, one settings row, up to 50 blocked sites, at most one calendar connection, one open draft and one running or paused session.
- A goal has one or more rules. A timed event belongs to at most one goal. A focus session belongs to at most one event. Events of one series share a `series_id`.
- A chat has any number of messages; each message belongs to exactly one chat and is deleted with it (added 17 Nov 2026, spec 44: asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge).
- An assistant message carries at most one draft plan. An accepted draft plan creates events (with their to-dos), moves or deletes the events it names and adds to-dos to the events it names; undoing it puts them back.
- An event has up to 20 to-dos. A to-do belongs to exactly one event (not to a series) and is deleted with it.
- An event of the user's own has up to 20 guests: e-mail addresses only, not accounts and not a list of the app's users. The guests are kept on the owner's event; nobody sees another user's rows (R3). A guest who uses the app with Outlook connected gets the event through their own Outlook sync, as any Outlook event.
- A goal has any number of tasks. A task belongs to at most one goal. A user has at most 100 open tasks.
- A user has at most one profile photo (added 15 Nov 2026 (spec 40, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge)).

## 3 Data model
Times in UTC, shown in the user's time zone. Lengths in minutes. Every table has `id` (uuid) and, except user, `user_id`.

- **user**: `email` text, `status` `unconfirmed`/`confirmed` (kept by Supabase Auth), `plan` `free`/`pro`, `stripe_customer_id` text or empty, `chat_count_date` date and `chat_count` integer (3; no longer used since spec 44, kept for older builds), `extension_seen_at` timestamp or empty, `display_name` text 1 to 40 characters after trimming, without control or invisible characters and with at least one letter or digit, or empty (then the part of the e-mail before the "@" is shown; "Lea Martins"), `bio` text 1 to 160 characters after trimming, on one line, without control or invisible characters, or empty ("Studying statistics at Nova"), `linkedin_url` exactly `https://www.linkedin.com/in/` and 3 to 100 characters of letters, digits, "-", "_" and "%", or empty ("https://www.linkedin.com/in/lea-martins") (added 15 Nov 2026 (spec 40, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge))
- **profile_photo** (added 15 Nov 2026 (spec 40, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge)): `user_id` → user (one per user), `mime` `image/jpeg`/`image/png`/`image/webp`, decided from the first bytes, `bytes` 1 to 200,000 bytes (the browser sends a 256 × 256 square), `updated_at` timestamp
- **contact_invitation** (added 18 Nov 2026, spec 53, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): `from_user` → user, `to_email` the address the sender typed, lower-case ("lea@example.com"), `to_user` → user or empty (empty when the address has no account, after a decline, or while a decline blocks the sender; such a row is seen only by its sender, who never sees `to_user`, R52), `created_at` timestamp; once per sender and address
- **contact** (added 18 Nov 2026, spec 53, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): `user_a`, `user_b` → user (the smaller id first, one row per pair), `created_at` timestamp
- **contact_decline** (added 18 Nov 2026, spec 53, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): `from_user`, `to_user` → user, `until` timestamp (30 days after the decline); seen by nobody, only checked by `invite_contact`
- **contact_invite_attempt** (added 18 Nov 2026, spec 53, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): `user_id` → user, `at` timestamp; one row per invitation sent, seen by nobody, only counted by `invite_contact` (R52's 10 per hour)
- **share** (added 18 Nov 2026, spec 53, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): `contact_id` → contact (deleted with it), `owner`, `viewer` → user (the two people of the contact, never the same), `calendar` `none`/`busy`/`details` (`none`), `focus` boolean (false; kept as it is, R55), `updated_at` timestamp; once per owner and viewer. No row means nothing is shared
- **settings**: `time_zone` text, from the browser at sign-up, or "Europe/Lisbon" if it gives none or an invalid one; changed in the settings by typing a city (the server finds the time zone at the city's position, without asking another service) or by taking the device's, `home_address` text or empty, `travel_mode` `driving`/`cycling`/`walking`/`transit` (public transport; default `driving`), `travel_buffer_minutes` integer 0 to 120 (10), `time_format` `12h`/`24h` (default `12h`), `day_start_hour` integer 0 to 23 (7) and `day_end_hour` integer 1 to 24 (21), the end after the start (the calendar's visible hours), `default_view` `day`/`week`/`month` (default `week`; R37), `welcome_answered` boolean (false)
- **goal**: `title` text 1 to 80 characters, `deadline` date ("2027-03-14"), `auto_block` boolean (false), `color` one of the ten or empty (then calculated, R35), `icon` one of the ten (`rocket`), `why` text up to 200 characters or empty ("Stay fit, even when the semester gets busy."), `demo` boolean (false), `created_at`, `updated_at` timestamps; (added 16 Nov 2026, spec 41: decided in the team meeting of 16 Nov 2026; the team confirms with the merge): `open_ended` boolean (false; true: the goal has no end date), `category` `sport`/`uni`/`work`/`personal`/`other` or empty, `category_other` text 1 to 30 characters after trimming, on one line, without control or invisible characters, only with `other`, or empty ("Music"), `session_minutes` integer 15 to 240 in steps of 15 or empty (60), `day_part` `morning` (starts before 12:00)/`afternoon` (12:00 to before 17:00)/`evening` (from 17:00) or empty (any time), `avoid_days` the weekdays it is never done on, 0 to 6 of them, Monday = 1 to Sunday = 7, saved as their digits in rising order ("" none, "67" weekends), `location` text 1 to 200 characters after trimming or empty (at home or nowhere in particular; "Gym, Rua Augusta 10"). An open-ended goal still has a date in `deadline`: a placeholder, the user's today plus 364 days, written when the goal becomes open-ended and left alone by later edits. It exists only because older versions of the app read every goal's deadline as a date; the app treats an open-ended goal as having no deadline
- **rule**: `goal_id` → goal, `kind` `count`/`hours`, `target` decimal (2), `min_minutes` integer or empty, only for `count` (45)
- **task**: `title` text 1 to 100 characters, `goal_id` → goal or empty, `due_date` date or empty ("2026-11-20"), `status` `open`/`done`, `done_at` timestamp (empty while open), `demo` boolean (false), `created_at`, `updated_at`
- **event**: `title` text 1 to 100 characters, `all_day` boolean (false), `start_at`, `end_at` timestamps, empty if all-day ("2026-11-16 09:00", "11:00"), `first_day`, `last_day` dates, only if all-day, `goal_id` → goal or empty, `location` text or empty, `travel_mode` `driving`/`cycling`/`walking`/`transit` or empty (empty: the standard from the settings; R27), `may_overlap` boolean (false), `status` `planned`/`done`, `source` `app`/`assistant`/`outlook`, `series_id` uuid or empty, `outlook_id` text or empty, `demo` boolean (false), `guests` list of e-mail addresses or empty (never set: empty; for the user's own single events up to 20, each a plain address of at most 254 characters, lower-case, once; for an Outlook event its attendees, up to 100; "lea@example.com"), `organizer` e-mail address or empty (only for an Outlook event), `notes` text 1 to 2,000 characters or empty, without spaces at the end, never on an Outlook event ("Ask Ana about the exam") (added 14 Nov 2026, issue #5; the team confirms with the merge), `updated_at`
- **event_todo**: `event_id` → event (an event that is not from Outlook), `title` text 1 to 100 characters ("Read chapter 3"), `done` boolean (false), `position` integer, its place in the event's list from 0, `created_at` timestamp
- **focus_session**: `event_id` → event or empty, `planned_minutes` integer (60), `status` `running`/`paused`/`finished`, `started_at`, `paused_at` (empty while running), `paused_seconds` integer (600), `ended_at` (empty until finished), `rating` integer 0 to 5 or empty (4)
- **blocked_site**: `domain` text ("instagram.com")
- **blocked_attempt**: `domain` text, `attempted_at` timestamp ("2026-11-16 10:15")
- **chat** (added 17 Nov 2026, spec 44: asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): `title` text 1 to 80 characters (the first message on one line, cut; "Chat" when empty), `created_at` timestamp, `updated_at` timestamp (the time of its latest message)
- **chat_message**: `chat_id` → chat (spec 44), `role` `user`/`assistant`, `text` up to 4,000 characters, `created_at` timestamp
- **draft_plan**: `message_id` → chat_message, `proposed_events` list of (title, start_at, end_at, goal_id, to-dos: 0 to 20 titles), `changes` list of (event, its `updated_at`, `move` with new start_at and end_at, or `delete`; with the event's title and old times for showing it), `added_todos` list of (event, 1 to 20 to-do titles to add at the end of its list; with the event's title and times for showing it), `undo` (set when accepted: the events it created, the old times of the events it moved, the fields and to-dos of the events it deleted, the to-dos it added to existing events; written only by the database), `status` `open`/`accepted`/`rejected`/`replaced`/`undone`; 1 to 30 new events, changes and lists of to-dos for existing events together; `version` (0 as the assistant wrote it, one more for each `edit_draft`; above 0 the draft is "adjusted by the person" and the history sent to Claude says so)
- **calendar_connection**: `status` `connected`/`needs_reconnect`, `token_encrypted` text, `last_synced_at` timestamp
- Calculated: rule status `open`/`met`/`missed`, `focused_minutes`, `focus_score`, `blocking_on`, `blocking_until`, `leave_at`.

## 4 Statuses
- **user `status`**: starts `unconfirmed` (`sign_up`). → `confirmed` (`confirm_email`).
- **event**: starts `planned`. `planned` → `done` (`mark_event_done`) → `planned` (`mark_event_planned`). All-day events stay `planned`.
- **task**: starts `open` (`add_task`). `open` → `done` (`complete_task`) → `open` (`reopen_task`). A done task is deleted by itself 7 days after it was done (R33).
- **focus_session**: starts `running` (`start_session`). `running` ⇄ `paused` (`pause_session`, `resume_session`). `running` or `paused` → `finished` (`end_session`, or by itself, R13). `cancel_session`, or less than one focused minute, deletes the row.
- **draft_plan**: starts `open` (`send_message` with a draft answer). `open` → `accepted` (`accept_draft`), `rejected` (`reject_draft`) or `replaced` (the next draft answer). `accepted` → `undone` (`undo_draft`), final. `delete_chat` (its chat) or `clear_chat` deletes it.
- **calendar_connection**: starts `connected` (`connect_outlook`). → `needs_reconnect` when Microsoft refuses the stored access → `connected` (`connect_outlook`). `disconnect_outlook` deletes it.
- **user `plan`**: starts `free`. → `pro` when Stripe confirms payment → `free` when Stripe confirms the subscription ended.
- **rule status** (calculated per week): `open` → `met` or `missed` (R8).

## 6 Rules
**General**
- **R1** A rejected action changes nothing. An accepted action is saved at once, several rows together in one step (`create_goal`, `accept_draft`, a series). The server checks every input. Exception: a failed `send_message` (checks, or Claude down) saves nothing, not even the user message (spec 44: nothing is counted any more).
- **R2** A week always runs Monday 00:00 to Sunday 24:00 in the user's time zone. A timed event belongs to the day and week it starts in; an all-day event to each day from its first to its last day.

**Access and devices**
- **R3** The database returns and changes only the signed-in user's rows (row-level security). User A asking for user B's event gets "Not found"; A's edit or delete of B's row is rejected. Without sign-in, any data request gets the sign-in page or "401" and no data. The exceptions, all read only and nothing else (added 18 Nov 2026, spec 53, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): (1) R43: a user's contacts, and people the user invited while the invitation is open, see the user's display name, photo, e-mail address, bio, LinkedIn link and time zone; (2) R56: a contact the user shares their calendar with reads the times of the user's events, and at `details` also their titles and places; (3) R55: the two people of a share both see the share itself. Nothing of another user is ever written, and the tables' row-level security stays "own rows only": these exceptions go only through database functions that check the relation and return the reduced shape.
- **R4** Two devices of one account share one database. An edit or delete carrying an older `updated_at` than the row is rejected. The database allows one running or paused session per user and accepts a draft only once.
- **R5** Permissions are checked where the action happens, not only on the screen: a Free user calling the chat address directly is rejected.
- **R6** Only the server sets `user.plan`, never the user, not even through the database.

**Goals and rules**
- **R7** `count`: 1 to 14 events per week, optional `min_minutes` 15 to 240. `hours`: 0.5 to 60 hours per week, in steps of 0.5.
- **R8** Only `done` events of the goal count, for every rule of that goal. With `min_minutes`, only events at least that long count. `hours` adds the lengths. A week is `met` when the target is reached, `missed` when it ended unmet, otherwise `open`. The week the goal was created is never `missed`.
- **R9** Goal progress (changed 18 Nov 2026, as asked and approved by Emily; the team confirms with the merge): every goal looks the same. Next to the title, how far the goal is this week ("2/3", "1/6 h"), then a bar; per rule "This week: **2** of 3 sessions" or "This week: **1** of 6 h", the number done in bold; then one row with the 7 days of this week, Monday to Sunday, today ringed: a day on which a `done` event of the goal starts is filled in the goal's colour with a check; else a day that has ended with a `planned` event of the goal gets a red cross (planned, not done); else today or a later day with a `planned` event of the goal gets a dashed ring (planned); every other day stays empty. A rest day is never a cross. A week is `met` if all rules are met, `missed` if any is missed, else `open` (R8); past weeks are no longer shown on the goal. The countdown ("12 days left", "Deadline passed"; "No end date" for a goal without an end date) stays under the goal. Weeks after the deadline are not judged. Editing a rule changes past weeks too (accepted limit). A goal without an end date (added 16 Nov 2026, spec 41): its weeks run from the week of creation to the current week, at most the latest 12 weeks (for the coach); past weeks are judged as for any goal (R8).

**Events**
- **R10** Overlap is never a reason to reject; both are saved. If either has `may_overlap` = false, both get a conflict mark and the user decides. Ending exactly when the next starts is no overlap. Another user's shared events never count: they give no conflict mark and no overlap question (R57) (added 18 Nov 2026, spec 53, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge).
- **R11** Outlook events keep title, times, location, attendees and organizer from Outlook; the user can only set the goal and mark them done. The editor shows their attendees read-only, the organizer first.

**Focus sessions**
- **R12** A session lasts a whole number of minutes from 1 minute to 23 h 59 min. The app suggests 15, 30, 60, 90 and 120 minutes; any other length is chosen on a wheel of hours and minutes.
- **R13** `focused_minutes` = `ended_at` − `started_at` − `paused_seconds`, rounded down to whole minutes. A running session finishes by itself at the planned length, even with the browser closed: the server sets `ended_at` = `started_at` + planned minutes + `paused_seconds`. A paused session stays paused until the user resumes or ends it.
- **R14** Under one focused minute, or cancelled: not saved. Rating is optional; empty is not 0; 0 is a real rating.

**Blocking and the extension**
- **R15** Blocking is on when (a) a session is `running` (not paused), or (b) now is within a timed event (start ≤ now < end) whose goal has `auto_block` on. `blocking_until` is the latest of these ends. Tabs already open on a blocked site also go to the block page (not an attempt), which shows when blocking ends.
- **R16** The extension asks the app every minute and receives only the block list, `blocking_on` and `blocking_until`. It reports blocked attempts, holds no keys and uses the app's sign-in in the same Chrome browser; signed out, nothing is blocked. If it cannot reach the app, it keeps the last answer until `blocking_until`, then stops; attempts are sent later.
- **R17** Domains are cleaned before saving ("https://www.Instagram.com/reels" becomes "instagram.com"). No duplicates; at most 50.
- **R18** Limits: only page loads in Chrome on that computer are blocked; not phone apps or other programs, and no screen time. Blocking can start up to one minute late. The blocker is a help, not a lock: the user can end a session, switch off auto-block or edit the list any time; ending early lowers the score. If the extension has not asked for 2 minutes, the app shows "Blocker not connected: nothing is being blocked". Sessions still work.

**Focus score and analytics**
- **R19** Our definition, per finished session: 100 × focused minutes ÷ planned minutes, rounded, minus 5 per blocked attempt during the session, kept between 0 and 100. A day or week shows the average of its sessions; no sessions shows "–", not 0. The 7 Nov idea needed screen-time data a website cannot read.
- **R20** Analytics, per day and week: focused minutes, finished sessions, focus score, blocked attempts, most blocked sites.

**AI chat** (the screen calls it "Coach")
- **R21** Pro only, once Free and Pro are built; until then every signed-in user may use it. One server setting decides (`CHAT_PRO_ONLY`: `true` means Pro only). There is no daily limit on messages (added 17 Nov 2026, spec 44: asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge); until then it was 20 a day. The provider account has a spending limit.
- **R22** Sent to Claude with every message: the last 10 messages of the same chat (spec 44: a new chat starts without the others), goals with deadlines and rules, events from today to 14 days ahead (title, start, end, `may_overlap`, `all_day`), R31's totals, the time zone, the current local date and time, and `day_start_hour` and `day_end_hour` from the settings. An assistant message is sent with its draft's proposed events (with their to-dos) and its changes in words (title, old time, new time). Each event gets a short reference valid for that one message only ("e1") and a flag whether the coach may change it: only the user's own timed events that are not from Outlook (R11), not part of a series (R28), have no guests (R41; they would get update or cancellation mails nobody asked for) and have not started. A second flag says whether the coach may add to-dos to it: the user's own events that are not from Outlook (R11, R40) and have not ended before today, also events of a series, all-day events and events that have started (sent only when false); and its number of to-dos (the count only, sent when above 0). No id of an event is sent; the server turns a reference in the answer back into the event at once and keeps its id and `updated_at` in the draft. An assistant message's draft also carries its to-dos for existing events in words (the event's title, the to-dos). Not sent: email, password, locations, block list, payment data, tasks, the titles of the to-dos of events in the calendar, an event's notes (added 14 Nov 2026, issue #5; the team confirms with the merge), guests and organizers (not even whether an event has any; only the flag says it may not be changed). A goal that has a routine (R44) is also sent with, each only when set: its category (and the own word for other), the session length, the part of the day, the weekdays it is never done on (as names and as the dates of the 14 days), whether its place is away from home (a place that is not a web link; the place itself, its address or name, is never sent), and for the current and the next week the sessions still left (R45); a goal without a routine is sent as before (added 16 Nov 2026, spec 43: decided in the team meeting of 16 Nov 2026; the team confirms with the merge). Nothing of another user is ever sent, also not a calendar a contact shares with the user, nor their name (added 18 Nov 2026, spec 53, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge). The chat page says what is sent.
- **R23** An answer is a text (changes nothing) or a draft. Every proposed event must be timed, start between now and the end of the 14th day from today (on 16 Nov: until 30 Nov 24:00), start before it ends, last 15 minutes to 8 hours, not overlap another proposal or a timed event with `may_overlap` = false, and use only the user's goals; it may carry 0 to 20 to-dos, each 1 to 100 characters after trimming, only the things the user named for that event, in their words (R40); at most 30 new events and changes together. A change names an event by its reference (an unknown one fails), only an event the coach may change (R22), each at most once; a move keeps everything but the times, which obey `edit_event` (start before end, at most 24 hours) and start between now and the end of the 14th day. Overlaps are checked on the calendar as it would be after the whole draft: new and moved events must not overlap each other or a remaining timed event with `may_overlap` = false (an event with `may_overlap` = true never blocks; a moved or deleted event no longer blocks at its old time). To-dos for an existing event name it by its reference (an unknown one fails), only an event that takes to-dos from the coach (R22), each at most once and never one the draft deletes (a moved one may get them): 1 to 20 titles, each 1 to 100 characters after trimming, only the things the user named, in their words; the event's to-dos now plus the new ones at most 20; each such list counts as one of the 30. A new event that names a goal must not start on a weekday the goal is never done on (the weekday of its start in the user's time zone); moves of existing events, events without a goal and the user's own events in the calendar are not checked against it; checked on the answer, again on `accept_draft` against the goal as it is then, and when a block of the draft is dragged (added 16 Nov 2026, spec 43: decided in the team meeting of 16 Nov 2026; the team confirms with the merge). One failed check rejects the whole answer (R1).
- **R46** Searching chats (added 17 Nov 2026, spec 44: asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): the coach's list finds the user's own chats (R3) whose title or one of whose messages contains every word typed, ignoring case, the words as typed (no wildcards); at most 8 words and 100 characters; newest first, at most 30, each with an excerpt around the first hit; opening one shows the message found. A search changes nothing and nothing is sent to another service.
- **R24** Voice: the browser's own speech recognition, in English (the coach is English only and always answers in English (added 17 Nov 2026, spec 44: asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge)), fills the editable input field, in browsers that have it. While it listens, a wave next to the microphone shows how loud the voice is; it is worked out in the browser, nothing is recorded or sent. Our server receives text only.

**Payment, Outlook, travel**
- **R25** Stripe test mode only: if the secret key does not start with `sk_test_`, the server rejects it, payment stays off and the error is logged. The plan changes only on Stripe's signed message to our server, never from the browser's return page.
- **R26** Connecting asks for read and write access at once; declined means not connected. Then every event with `source` `app` or `assistant` that has not ended goes to Outlook, in batches, with later edits and deletions. If Outlook is down or refuses for now (too many requests), the event is marked "Not sent to Outlook" (empty `outlook_id`) and sent at the next sync. Events deleted in Outlook vanish at the next sync; its repeating events arrive as single events, with their attendees and organizer. An event with guests goes with them as required attendees (an empty list once all were removed); an event that never had guests is sent without attendees, as before. An event's notes are never sent to Outlook; they stay in the app (added 14 Nov 2026, issue #5; the team confirms with the merge). Disconnecting deletes imported events from the app; what the app wrote stays in Outlook.
- **R27** Travel time needs a timed event with a location that is not a web link and a home address (else "Add your home address in settings"). Google Maps for where the addresses are and for the way of getting there (by car with the traffic expected about an hour before the start; public transport: Google's timetables, the latest trip that arrives in time) (changed 16 Nov 2026, team decision: Google Maps instead of OpenStreetMap). The way is the event's own `travel_mode` when it has one (chosen in the event editor, for timed events of our own only), otherwise the standard from the settings (changed 13 Nov 2026, team decision); "Google Maps" is credited in the settings' Travel card and under the address suggestions (changed 16 Nov 2026). `leave_at` = start − travel minutes − buffer. With a time to leave, the editor offers "Show map": Google's map of the way from the home address to the location, with the event's way of getting there, loaded only when opened (added 16 Nov 2026, team decision). The app may reuse a result for up to 6 hours; it is never stored with the event. Address not found: "Address not found", event unchanged.

**Repeating and all-day events**
- **R28** Timed events can repeat `daily`, `weekly` (chosen weekdays, always including the first date's) or `monthly` (same day of the month; months without it are skipped) until `repeat_until`, which is on or after the first date. At most 100 dates. All are created at once as normal events with one `series_id` and the same title, time, length, goal, location and `may_overlap`; each has its own status and goes to Outlook singly. Our decision: no repeat rule is stored; to extend a series, create a new one. Scope `this_and_following` changes or deletes this event and the later ones of its series; edits keep their dates.
- **R29** An all-day event has a first and last day (at most 31 days), no times, and shows at the top of its days. It has no goal, never counts for rules, cannot be done, gets no conflict mark, no blocking, no travel time and no repeat. It syncs with Outlook like other events. The AI chat sees it but never proposes one.

**Accounts**
- **R30** Supabase Auth sends confirmation and reset links; only `confirmed` users can sign in. The neutral answers of `resend_confirmation` and `request_password_reset` stop anyone testing which emails have accounts. ## 7 What is saved, where, and who can see it
- **Email, password:** Supabase Auth, the password only as a hash (it cannot be turned back into the password).
- **All rows of section 3:** Supabase (Postgres) with row-level security. Seen only by the owner.
- **Outlook access:** encrypted in `calendar_connection`, read only by our server; never in the browser or extension.
- **Card details:** Stripe only; we keep `stripe_customer_id` and `plan`.
- **AI chat:** chats (with their titles), messages and drafts saved; R22's data goes to the provider of Claude.
- **Tasks:** saved in the database, visible only to their user (R3).
- **To-dos inside an event:** saved in the database with their event, visible only to their user (R3); never sent to Outlook.
- **Guests of an event (R41):** e-mail addresses of other people, so personal data. Saved only in the owner's event (also an Outlook event's attendees and organizer), shown only to the owner (R3), sent only to Outlook (which invites them), never logged, never sent to Claude (R22).
- **Profile** (added 15 Nov 2026 (spec 40, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge)): the display name, photo, bio and LinkedIn link are personal data the user typed or chose, in the database; shown only to the user (R43); since spec 53 also to their contacts and the people they invited (R43) (changed 18 Nov 2026, spec 53, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge). Never sent to Claude or Outlook, never logged. Deleted with the account.
- **Contacts and invitations** (added 18 Nov 2026, spec 53, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): e-mail addresses of other users and addresses the user typed, so personal data. In the database with row-level security; a contact's address is seen by the two people only; a typed address only by its sender (R52). Never logged, never sent to Claude. Deleted with the account (the invitations someone sent to it stay in that sender's list as the address they typed).
- **Shares** (added 18 Nov 2026, spec 53, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): in the database with row-level security, seen by the owner and the viewer only, deleted with the contact or either account. What leaves the owner's rows, and only to that viewer, only while the share and the contact last: at `busy` the times of their events (merged), at `details` also titles and places, Outlook events included (R56). Computed at each request, never copied, never logged, never sent to Claude or Outlook. The viewer's browser remembers only which people's calendars to show (their user ids, under a key of the signed-in user's own), never their events.
- **A goal's routine** (added 16 Nov 2026, spec 41: decided in the team meeting of 16 Nov 2026; the team confirms with the merge): saved in the database with the goal; seen only by the user (R3). Its location is an address the user typed, so personal data: never logged, not sent to Claude (R22) or Outlook. Since spec 43 the rest of the routine goes to the provider of Claude with each message (R22): the category, the session length, the part of the day, the never days, whether the place is away from home (a yes, never the place) and the sessions left; an accepted coach session of the goal gets the place as its location, exactly as if the user had typed it into the event (R27) (added 16 Nov 2026, spec 43: decided in the team meeting of 16 Nov 2026; the team confirms with the merge).
- **Widgets and weather** (added 17 Nov 2026, spec 47, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): the widget list is saved in `settings`, seen only by the user (R3). The device's location is used once per weather question, rounded to about 1 km, and never saved or logged (R48); only those rounded coordinates go to Open-Meteo.
- **Extension:** Chrome's storage holds the block list and blocking state, on that computer only.

**Secrets:** Claude API key, Supabase service key and connection string, Microsoft client secret, the key that encrypts the Outlook access, the routing service key, Stripe secret and webhook keys, the e-mail sender's password. Only on the server as environment variables, never in the public repository, the browser or the extension. After a leak: revoke the key, create a new one, check the provider's usage.

## 9 Open decisions
Each with its default.
- Public transport for travel time? Offered as a travel mode (13 Nov 2026, team decision); the timetables come from Google Maps (16 Nov 2026; before that from the free Transitous service).
- Price of Pro? €4.99 per month, test mode, no trial.

- Does finishing a session mark its event done? No, the user marks it.

## 10 Out of scope
Booking-page integrations; chat-tool status; calendar subscription links (ICS); coach recommendations, an evening check-in and a weekly review (our prototype had them); admin accounts; sharing beyond profiles, contacts and calendars (R43, R52 to R58), namely shared to-do lists, event invitations, sharing the focus chart, hover cards on people's names, editing, moving or answering other people's events, public links to a calendar, sharing with groups, inviting people who are not users yet (no e-mail is sent) and blocking a user (changed 18 Nov 2026, spec 53, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge); other social features (guests, R41, are not sharing: Outlook invites them and nobody sees another user's rows); guests' answers (accepted, declined) and answering invitations in the app; choosing guests from a list of people or a team; guests' names or pictures; a native phone app; calendars other than Outlook; other sign-in methods; habit, sport and cycle tracking; dark mode; games; screen-time measurement; travel stopovers; sub-tasks; repeating tasks; putting a task into the calendar (seeing other users' profiles was here from 15 Nov 2026, spec 40, until spec 53).
