A web app that brings calendar, goals and focused work together, and blocks distracting websites in Chrome.
Rule for coding agents: read this file first. Do not change it without the team's approval; propose the change and wait.

## Purpose and scope
- **Problem and user.** Anyone who wants to be more productive and is constantly distracted while planning across three or four tools: students, employees, self-employed people, in private life or at work. Example persona: a student in the exam phase (lectures, a statistics exam, half-marathon training).
- **User journey.** Sign up, set goals with weekly rules, note tasks, plan days, focus while sites are blocked, mark events done, see progress.
- **One role:** the signed-in user. No admin. Nothing is shared between users, except that contacts see each other's display name, photo and e-mail address (R42 to R46), and also bio, LinkedIn link and time zone (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 31, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge) (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 28, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge), and what a user chooses to share with one of their contacts: their calendar, free/busy or with details, read only, and their focus chart (R47 to R51) (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 29, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge).
- **Two plans:** Free has everything except the AI chat; Pro adds it, paid through Stripe in test mode.

## 2 Relationships
- Every saved row belongs to one user, who has one plan, one settings row, up to 50 blocked sites, at most one calendar connection, one open draft and one running or paused session.
- A goal has one or more rules. A timed event belongs to at most one goal. A focus session belongs to at most one event. Events of one series share a `series_id`.
- An assistant message carries at most one draft plan. An accepted draft plan creates events (with their to-dos), moves or deletes the events it names and adds to-dos to the events it names; undoing it puts them back.
- An event has up to 20 to-dos. A to-do belongs to exactly one event (not to a series) and is deleted with it.
- An event of the user's own has up to 20 guests: e-mail addresses only, not accounts and not a list of the app's users. The guests are kept on the owner's event; nobody sees another user's rows (R3). A guest who uses the app with Outlook connected gets the event through their own Outlook sync, as any Outlook event.
- A goal has any number of tasks. A task belongs to at most one goal. A user has at most 100 open tasks.
- A contact has at most two shares, one in each direction; a share belongs to its owner and hangs on the contact, so it ends with it. Sharing is one-directional (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 29, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge).
- A user has at most one profile photo, at most 20 open invitations sent and at most 100 contacts. An invitation goes from one user to one e-mail address; a contact joins exactly two users, one row per pair, and belongs to both; either may end it (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 28, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge).
- A user owns at most 20 lists. A list has one owner, up to 20 members and up to 200 entries. A membership belongs to one list and one user and hangs on the contact row between owner and member: when that contact ends, the membership ends. An entry belongs to exactly one list and is deleted with it (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 30, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge) (withdrawn 15 Nov 2026 (spec 34, decided by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): not part of the app; the tables are kept empty and closed).

## 3 Data model
Times in UTC, shown in the user's time zone. Lengths in minutes. Every table has `id` (uuid) and, except user, `user_id`.

- **user**: `email` text, `status` `unconfirmed`/`confirmed` (kept by Supabase Auth), `plan` `free`/`pro`, `stripe_customer_id` text or empty, `chat_count_date` date and `chat_count` integer (3), `extension_seen_at` timestamp or empty, `display_name` text 1 to 40 characters after trimming, without control characters, or empty (then the part of the e-mail before the "@" is shown; "Lea Martins") (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 28, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge), `bio` text 1 to 160 characters after trimming on one line, under the rule of a list title, or empty ("Studying statistics at Nova"), `linkedin_url` exactly `https://www.linkedin.com/in/` and 3 to 100 characters of letters, digits, "-", "_" and "%", or empty ("https://www.linkedin.com/in/lea-martins") (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 31, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge), `demo_contacts` boolean (false; whether the user sees the demo contacts, R58) (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 32, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge). The time zone others see is the one in `settings`, never copied
- **settings**: `time_zone` text, from the browser at sign-up, or "Europe/Lisbon" if it gives none or an invalid one; changed in the settings by typing a city (the server finds the time zone at the city's position, without asking another service) or by taking the device's, `home_address` text or empty, `travel_mode` `driving`/`cycling`/`walking`/`transit` (public transport; default `driving`), `travel_buffer_minutes` integer 0 to 120 (10), `time_format` `12h`/`24h` (default `12h`), `day_start_hour` integer 0 to 23 (7) and `day_end_hour` integer 1 to 24 (21), the end after the start (the calendar's visible hours), `default_view` `day`/`week`/`month` (default `week`; R37), `welcome_answered` boolean (false)
- **goal**: `title` text 1 to 80 characters, `deadline` date ("2027-03-14"), `auto_block` boolean (false), `color` one of the ten or empty (then calculated, R35), `icon` one of the ten (`rocket`), `why` text up to 200 characters or empty ("Stay fit, even when the semester gets busy."), `demo` boolean (false), `created_at`, `updated_at` timestamps
- **rule**: `goal_id` → goal, `kind` `count`/`hours`, `target` decimal (2), `min_minutes` integer or empty, only for `count` (45)
- **task**: `title` text 1 to 100 characters, `goal_id` → goal or empty, `due_date` date or empty ("2026-11-20"), `status` `open`/`done`, `done_at` timestamp (empty while open), `demo` boolean (false), `created_at`, `updated_at`
- **event**: `title` text 1 to 100 characters, `all_day` boolean (false), `start_at`, `end_at` timestamps, empty if all-day ("2026-11-16 09:00", "11:00"), `first_day`, `last_day` dates, only if all-day, `goal_id` → goal or empty, `location` text or empty, `travel_mode` `driving`/`cycling`/`walking`/`transit` or empty (empty: the standard from the settings; R27), `may_overlap` boolean (false), `status` `planned`/`done`, `source` `app`/`assistant`/`outlook`, `series_id` uuid or empty, `outlook_id` text or empty, `demo` boolean (false), `guests` list of e-mail addresses or empty (never set: empty; for the user's own single events up to 20, each a plain address of at most 254 characters, lower-case, once; for an Outlook event its attendees, up to 100; "lea@example.com"), `organizer` e-mail address or empty (only for an Outlook event), `notes` text 1 to 2,000 characters or empty, without spaces at the end, never on an Outlook event ("Ask Ana about the exam") (added 14 Nov 2026, issue #5; the team confirms with the merge), `updated_at`
- **event_todo**: `event_id` → event (an event that is not from Outlook), `title` text 1 to 100 characters ("Read chapter 3"), `done` boolean (false), `position` integer, its place in the event's list from 0, `created_at` timestamp
- **focus_session**: `event_id` → event or empty, `planned_minutes` integer (60), `status` `running`/`paused`/`finished`, `started_at`, `paused_at` (empty while running), `paused_seconds` integer (600), `ended_at` (empty until finished), `rating` integer 0 to 5 or empty (4)
- **blocked_site**: `domain` text ("instagram.com")
- **blocked_attempt**: `domain` text, `attempted_at` timestamp ("2026-11-16 10:15")
- **chat_message**: `role` `user`/`assistant`, `text` up to 4,000 characters, `created_at` timestamp
- **draft_plan**: `message_id` → chat_message, `proposed_events` list of (title, start_at, end_at, goal_id, to-dos: 0 to 20 titles), `changes` list of (event, its `updated_at`, `move` with new start_at and end_at, or `delete`; with the event's title and old times for showing it), `added_todos` list of (event, 1 to 20 to-do titles to add at the end of its list; with the event's title and times for showing it), `undo` (set when accepted: the events it created, the old times of the events it moved, the fields and to-dos of the events it deleted, the to-dos it added to existing events; written only by the database), `status` `open`/`accepted`/`rejected`/`replaced`/`undone`; 1 to 30 new events, changes and lists of to-dos for existing events together; `version` (0 as the assistant wrote it, one more for each `edit_draft`; above 0 the draft is "adjusted by the person" and the history sent to Claude says so)
- **calendar_connection**: `status` `connected`/`needs_reconnect`, `token_encrypted` text, `last_synced_at` timestamp
- **profile_photo** (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 28, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): `user_id` → user (one per user), `mime` `image/jpeg`/`image/png`/`image/webp`, decided from the first bytes, `bytes` 1 to 200,000 bytes (the browser sends a 256 × 256 square), `updated_at` timestamp
- **contact_invitation** (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 28, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): `from_user` → user, `to_email` the address the sender typed, lower-case ("lea@example.com"), `to_user` → user or empty (empty when the address has no account, after a decline, or while a decline blocks the sender; such a row is seen only by its sender, who never sees `to_user`, R44), `created_at` timestamp; once per sender and address
- **contact** (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 28, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): `user_a`, `user_b` → user (the smaller id first, one row per pair), `created_at` timestamp
- **list** (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 30, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge) (withdrawn 15 Nov 2026 (spec 34, decided by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): not part of the app; the tables are kept empty and closed): `owner` → user, `title` text 1 to 60 characters after trimming, without control characters ("Flat shopping"), `created_at`, `updated_at` timestamps (`updated_at` changes with the title only)
- **list_member** (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 30, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge) (withdrawn 15 Nov 2026 (spec 34, decided by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): not part of the app; the tables are kept empty and closed): `list_id` → list, `user_id` → user, `contact_id` → contact (the row between the owner and this user), `added_at` timestamp; once per list and user; never the owner
- **list_entry** (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 30, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge) (withdrawn 15 Nov 2026 (spec 34, decided by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): not part of the app; the tables are kept empty and closed): `list_id` → list, `title` text 1 to 200 characters after trimming, without control characters ("Oat milk"), `done` boolean (false), `position` integer, its place in the list from 0, `added_by` → user or empty (empty once that account is deleted), `done_by` → user or empty (who ticked it; empty while open), `created_at`, `updated_at` timestamps (`updated_at` changes with the title, the tick and the entry's own move)
- **contact_decline** (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 28, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): `from_user`, `to_user` → user, `until` timestamp (30 days after the decline); seen by nobody, only checked by `invite_contact`
- **share** (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 29, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): `contact_id` → contact (deleted with it), `owner`, `viewer` → user (the two people of the contact, never the same), `calendar` `none`/`busy`/`details` (`none`), `focus` boolean (false), `updated_at` timestamp; once per owner and viewer. No row means nothing is shared; sharing nothing removes the row
- Calculated: rule status `open`/`met`/`missed`, `focused_minutes`, `focus_score`, `blocking_on`, `blocking_until`, `leave_at`.

## 4 Statuses
- **user `status`**: starts `unconfirmed` (`sign_up`). → `confirmed` (`confirm_email`).
- **event**: starts `planned`. `planned` → `done` (`mark_event_done`) → `planned` (`mark_event_planned`). All-day events stay `planned`.
- **task**: starts `open` (`add_task`). `open` → `done` (`complete_task`) → `open` (`reopen_task`). A done task is deleted by itself 7 days after it was done (R33).
- **focus_session**: starts `running` (`start_session`). `running` ⇄ `paused` (`pause_session`, `resume_session`). `running` or `paused` → `finished` (`end_session`, or by itself, R13). `cancel_session`, or less than one focused minute, deletes the row.
- **draft_plan**: starts `open` (`send_message` with a draft answer). `open` → `accepted` (`accept_draft`), `rejected` (`reject_draft`) or `replaced` (the next draft answer). `accepted` → `undone` (`undo_draft`), final. `clear_chat` deletes it.
- **calendar_connection**: starts `connected` (`connect_outlook`). → `needs_reconnect` when Microsoft refuses the stored access → `connected` (`connect_outlook`). `disconnect_outlook` deletes it.
- **user `plan`**: starts `free`. → `pro` when Stripe confirms payment → `free` when Stripe confirms the subscription ended.
- **rule status** (calculated per week): `open` → `met` or `missed` (R8).

## 5 Actions
The first six actions need no sign-in; all others need it and touch only the user's own rows (R3; the exceptions are R43's: what contacts and invited users see, and R48's and R49's: what a viewer reads of a share, never writes). Rejected actions change nothing (R1).

| `confirm_email` | link | Link valid and unused | `confirmed` | "This link has expired. Ask for a new one" |

## 6 Rules
**General**
- **R1** A rejected action changes nothing. An accepted action is saved at once, several rows together in one step (`create_goal`, `accept_draft`, a series). The server checks every input. Exception: a failed `send_message` (checks, or Claude down) saves nothing, not even the user message, but counts towards R21.
- **R2** A week always runs Monday 00:00 to Sunday 24:00 in the user's time zone. A timed event belongs to the day and week it starts in; an all-day event to each day from its first to its last day.

**Access and devices**
- **R3** The database returns and changes only the signed-in user's rows (row-level security). User A asking for user B's event gets "Not found"; A's edit or delete of B's row is rejected. Without sign-in, any data request gets the sign-in page or "401" and no data. The exceptions, all read only and nothing else (reworded 15 Nov 2026, spec 29, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): (1) R43: a user's contacts, and people the user invited, see the user's display name, photo and e-mail address (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 28), bio, LinkedIn link and time zone (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 31, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge); (2) R48: a contact the user shares their calendar with reads the times of the user's events, and at `details` also their titles and places; (3) R49: a contact the user shares their focus chart with reads its weekly and daily totals; (4) R47: the two people of a share both see the share itself. Nothing of another user is ever written, and the tables' row-level security stays "own rows only": these exceptions go only through database functions that check the relation and return the reduced shape. (A further exception for the people of a shared list, R55, was withdrawn 15 Nov 2026 (spec 34, decided by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): not part of the app; the tables are kept empty and closed.)
- **R4** Two devices of one account share one database. An edit or delete carrying an older `updated_at` than the row is rejected. The database allows one running or paused session per user and accepts a draft only once.
- **R5** Permissions are checked where the action happens, not only on the screen: a Free user calling the chat address directly is rejected.
- **R6** Only the server sets `user.plan`, never the user, not even through the database.

**Goals and rules**
- **R7** `count`: 1 to 14 events per week, optional `min_minutes` 15 to 240. `hours`: 0.5 to 60 hours per week, in steps of 0.5.
- **R8** Only `done` events of the goal count, for every rule of that goal. With `min_minutes`, only events at least that long count. `hours` adds the lengths. A week is `met` when the target is reached, `missed` when it ended unmet, otherwise `open`. The week the goal was created is never `missed`.
- **R9** Goal progress: per rule "This week: 1 of 2 done" or "3.5 of 6 h"; a strip of weeks from creation to deadline (`met` if all rules are met, `missed` if any is missed, else `open`); a countdown ("12 days left", "Deadline passed"). Weeks after the deadline are not judged. Editing a rule changes past weeks too (accepted limit).

**Events**
- **R10** Overlap is never a reason to reject; both are saved. If either has `may_overlap` = false, both get a conflict mark and the user decides. Ending exactly when the next starts is no overlap.
- **R11** Outlook events keep title, times, location, attendees and organizer from Outlook; the user can only set the goal and mark them done. The editor shows their attendees read-only, the organizer first.

**Focus sessions**
- **R12** A session lasts a whole number of minutes from 1 minute to 23 h 59 min. The app suggests 15, 30, 60, 90 and 120 minutes; any other length is chosen on a wheel of hours and minutes.
- **R13** `focused_minutes` = `ended_at` − `started_at` − `paused_seconds`, rounded down to whole minutes. A running session finishes by itself at the planned length, even with the browser closed: the server sets `ended_at` = `started_at` + planned minutes + `paused_seconds`. A paused session stays paused until the user resumes or ends it.
- **R14** Under one focused minute, or cancelled: not saved. Rating is optional; empty is not 0; 0 is a real rating.

**Blocking and the extension**
- **R15** Blocking is on when (a) a session is `running` (not paused), or (b) now is within a timed event (start ≤ now < end) whose goal has `auto_block` on. `blocking_until` is the latest of these ends. Tabs already open on a blocked site also go to the block page (not an attempt), which shows when blocking ends.
- **R16** The extension asks the app every minute and receives only the block list, `blocking_on` and `blocking_until`. It reports blocked attempts, holds no keys and uses the app's sign-in in the same Chrome browser; signed out, nothing is blocked. If it cannot reach the app, it keeps the last answer until `blocking_until`, then stops; attempts are sent later.
- **R17** Domains are cleaned before saving ("https://www.Instagram.com/reels" becomes "instagram.com"). No duplicates; at most 50.
- **R18** Limits: only page loads in Chrome on that computer are blocked; not phone apps or other programs, and no screen time. Blocking can start up to one minute late. The blocker is a help, not a lock: the user can end a session, switch off auto-block or edit the list any time; ending early lowers the score. If the extension has not asked for 2 minutes, the app shows "Blocker not connected: nothing is being blocked". Sessions still work.

**Focus score and analytics**
- **R19** Our definition, per finished session: 100 × focused minutes ÷ planned minutes, rounded, minus 5 per blocked attempt during the session, kept between 0 and 100. A day or week shows the average of its sessions; no sessions shows "–", not 0. The 7 Nov idea needed screen-time data a website cannot read.
- **R20** Analytics, per day and week: focused minutes, finished sessions, focus score, blocked attempts, most blocked sites.

**AI chat** (the screen calls it "Coach")
- **R21** Pro only, once Free and Pro are built; until then every signed-in user may use it. One server setting decides (`CHAT_PRO_ONLY`: `true` means Pro only). At most 20 messages (`send_message`) per user per calendar day in the user's time zone, counted in the database. The provider account has a spending limit.
- **R22** Sent to Claude with every message: the last 10 messages, goals with deadlines and rules, events from today to 14 days ahead (title, start, end, `may_overlap`, `all_day`), R31's totals, the time zone, the current local date and time, and `day_start_hour` and `day_end_hour` from the settings. An assistant message is sent with its draft's proposed events (with their to-dos) and its changes in words (title, old time, new time). Each event gets a short reference valid for that one message only ("e1") and a flag whether the coach may change it: only the user's own timed events that are not from Outlook (R11), not part of a series (R28), have no guests (R41; they would get update or cancellation mails nobody asked for) and have not started. A second flag says whether the coach may add to-dos to it: the user's own events that are not from Outlook (R11, R40) and have not ended before today, also events of a series, all-day events and events that have started (sent only when false); and its number of to-dos (the count only, sent when above 0). No id of an event is sent; the server turns a reference in the answer back into the event at once and keeps its id and `updated_at` in the draft. An assistant message's draft also carries its to-dos for existing events in words (the event's title, the to-dos). Not sent: email, password, locations, block list, payment data, tasks, the titles of the to-dos of events in the calendar, an event's notes (added 14 Nov 2026, issue #5; the team confirms with the merge), guests and organizers (not even whether an event has any; only the flag says it may not be changed) (shared lists and their entries were here, R57, until they were withdrawn 15 Nov 2026, spec 34, decided by the owner; the team confirms with the merge). Nothing of another user is ever sent, also not a calendar or focus chart a contact shares with the user, nor their name (confirmed 15 Nov 2026, spec 29, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge). The chat page says what is sent.
- **R23** An answer is a text (changes nothing) or a draft. Every proposed event must be timed, start between now and the end of the 14th day from today (on 16 Nov: until 30 Nov 24:00), start before it ends, last 15 minutes to 8 hours, not overlap another proposal or a timed event with `may_overlap` = false, and use only the user's goals; it may carry 0 to 20 to-dos, each 1 to 100 characters after trimming, only the things the user named for that event, in their words (R40); at most 30 new events and changes together. A change names an event by its reference (an unknown one fails), only an event the coach may change (R22), each at most once; a move keeps everything but the times, which obey `edit_event` (start before end, at most 24 hours) and start between now and the end of the 14th day. Overlaps are checked on the calendar as it would be after the whole draft: new and moved events must not overlap each other or a remaining timed event with `may_overlap` = false (an event with `may_overlap` = true never blocks; a moved or deleted event no longer blocks at its old time). To-dos for an existing event name it by its reference (an unknown one fails), only an event that takes to-dos from the coach (R22), each at most once and never one the draft deletes (a moved one may get them): 1 to 20 titles, each 1 to 100 characters after trimming, only the things the user named, in their words; the event's to-dos now plus the new ones at most 20; each such list counts as one of the 30. One failed check rejects the whole answer (R1).
- **R24** Voice: the browser's own speech recognition, English or German (chosen next to the microphone), fills the editable input field, in browsers that have it. Our server receives text only.

**Payment, Outlook, travel**
- **R25** Stripe test mode only: if the secret key does not start with `sk_test_`, the server rejects it, payment stays off and the error is logged. The plan changes only on Stripe's signed message to our server, never from the browser's return page.
- **R26** Connecting asks for read and write access at once; declined means not connected. Then every event with `source` `app` or `assistant` that has not ended goes to Outlook, in batches, with later edits and deletions. If Outlook is down or refuses for now (too many requests), the event is marked "Not sent to Outlook" (empty `outlook_id`) and sent at the next sync. Events deleted in Outlook vanish at the next sync; its repeating events arrive as single events, with their attendees and organizer. An event with guests goes with them as required attendees (an empty list once all were removed); an event that never had guests is sent without attendees, as before. An event's notes are never sent to Outlook; they stay in the app (added 14 Nov 2026, issue #5; the team confirms with the merge). Disconnecting deletes imported events from the app; what the app wrote stays in Outlook.
- **R27** Travel time needs a timed event with a location that is not a web link and a home address (else "Add your home address in settings"). OpenStreetMap routing for the way of getting there (public transport: timetable data). The way is the event's own `travel_mode` when it has one (chosen in the event editor, for timed events of our own only), otherwise the standard from the settings (changed 13 Nov 2026, team decision); "© OpenStreetMap contributors" is shown once, in the settings' Travel card (changed 13 Nov 2026). `leave_at` = start − travel minutes − buffer. The app may reuse a result for up to 6 hours; it is never stored with the event. Address not found: "Address not found", event unchanged. The event preview also shows the place on a map (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 35, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): our server finds where the address is (the same look-up as for the travel time, reused for up to 6 hours, at most 60 questions per user per minute, `read_event_place`); only while the preview is open for an event with an address does the browser load OpenStreetMap's own map with those coordinates, with "Open in OpenStreetMap" and "© OpenStreetMap contributors" under it. The map's address is built from the two numbers only. A web link has no map (an https link is shown as a link); a place that is not found shows "Address not found on the map" and no map. Other people's shared events show their place as text only and are never looked up. The preview shows the travel line when it can be worked out, or the hint to add a home address.

**Repeating and all-day events**
- **R28** Timed events can repeat `daily`, `weekly` (chosen weekdays, always including the first date's) or `monthly` (same day of the month; months without it are skipped) until `repeat_until`, which is on or after the first date. At most 100 dates. All are created at once as normal events with one `series_id` and the same title, time, length, goal, location and `may_overlap`; each has its own status and goes to Outlook singly. Our decision: no repeat rule is stored; to extend a series, create a new one. Scope `this_and_following` changes or deletes this event and the later ones of its series; edits keep their dates.
- **R29** An all-day event has a first and last day (at most 31 days), no times, and shows at the top of its days. It has no goal, never counts for rules, cannot be done, gets no conflict mark, no blocking, no travel time and no repeat. It syncs with Outlook like other events. The AI chat sees it but never proposes one.

**Accounts**
- **R30** Supabase Auth sends confirmation and reset links; only `confirmed` users can sign in. The neutral answers of `resend_confirmation` and `request_password_reset` stop anyone testing which emails have accounts. Limit: Supabase's built-in sender allows 2 e-mails per hour for the whole project (section 9).

**AI chat: questions about the past**
- **R31** For every message the server, not Claude, adds up the current week and the 7 before: per week and goal, minutes of `done` timed events and focused minutes of finished sessions (a session counts for its event's goal, else "no goal"); for `done` events without a goal, minutes per title, only the 20 titles with the most minutes. Limit: the assistant sees only these totals, no single past events, nothing older than 8 weeks.

**Display**
- **R34** Times are shown as the user's `time_format` says: 12-hour by default ("9am", "1:30pm", a range "9 – 10:30am", an end with its length "1:30pm · 1h"), or 24-hour ("9:00", "13:30"). The time pickers in the New event card are a 24-hour wheel (hours 00 to 23, minutes 00 to 59) or a typed "HH:MM"; anything else is refused with a message, and the latest end is 23:59 (13 Nov 2026, team decision). Dates are month-first ("Nov 16", "Mon, Nov 16", "Monday, November 16"). The calendar shows the hours from `day_start_hour` to `day_end_hour`, widened when an event starts outside them. Only the screen changes: stored values, the values of form fields and server messages keep their form.
- **R37** The calendar shows one of three views: a day, a week (Monday to Sunday) or a month (six weeks from the Monday on or before the 1st). The address holds the view and the day (`/?view=day&date=2026-11-18`), so reload and back work; without them the calendar opens with `default_view` on today. Previous and next move by a day, a week or a month (kept inside the month: Jan 31 goes to Feb 28). Each view reads only its days; every event shows on each day it touches (an all-day event on each of its days, an event past midnight also on the next day, without its start time). Phones get day and month only; a week's address shows its day there. Keys: D, W, M for the views, T for today, ← → for previous and next, never while typing or while a dialog is open.
- **R38** Dragging in the day and week grid is only a quicker way to the same actions: dragging on free time opens the new-event card (`create_event`); moving a block or dragging its bottom edge is `edit_event` with the event's `updated_at` (R4), snapped to 15 minutes, never past the day's midnight; Outlook events (R11), all-day events and the part of an event after midnight cannot be dragged. For 8 seconds after a move, a resize or a delete, "Undo" sends `edit_event` back to the old times, or `create_event` with the deleted event's fields, to-dos and guests (it gets a new id; a done event is marked done again; done to-dos are done again; Outlook invites the guests again). A refusal shows the action's message and the screen goes back (R1). The blocks of an open draft that it creates or moves can be dragged the same way in the chat (up and down, the bottom edge, snapped to 15 minutes, on their day); each drop is `edit_draft`, and the calendar changes only when the draft is applied.
- **R39** The editor of an existing event saves with a Save button: a change shows in the editor only, and "Save" and "Discard" appear in the footer as soon as a field differs from the saved event (never for an unchanged event, never enabled for an empty title). "Save" is `edit_event` with the event's `updated_at` (R4); the calendar shows the change at once, and a refusal shows the action's message and keeps the fields as typed (R1: nothing was saved). "Discard" puts the saved values back; closing the editor drops changes that were not saved. An event of a series first asks for the scope (R28). "Mark done", "Reopen", the bin and every change of the to-dos (R40) are sent at once. A new event is created with `create_event` (the new-event card, or "Add event" in the editor), then edited the same way.

**To-dos inside an event**
- **R40** An event that is not from Outlook (R11; timed or all-day) has up to 20 to-dos: a title of 1 to 100 characters, done or not, and a place in the event's list. A to-do belongs to exactly one event, not to a series (a series created with to-dos gets them on every event, each its own), and is deleted with it. Each change (`add_todo`, `rename_todo`, `tick_todo`, `move_todo`, `delete_todo`) is saved at once, not with the editor's Save button (R39), and does not change the event's `updated_at` (R4). The database checks the limit, also for two additions at the same moment, and refuses to-dos on Outlook events. To-dos are never sent to Outlook (R26) and never count for a rule. The editor shows them under the time with the count ("2/3"); a block tall enough shows the count too. The coach writes to-dos into the new events of a draft, and adds them at the end of the list of an event already in the calendar, when the user lists things to do during an event (R23); it does not tick, rename or remove to-dos.

**Guests**
- **R41** A single event of the user's own (`source` `app` or `assistant`, timed or all-day; not an event of a series, not a demo event, not an Outlook event, R11) may have up to 20 guests: plain e-mail addresses, at most 254 characters each, lower-cased, each once; no names, no accounts. Outlook does the inviting: guests are set or changed only while Outlook is connected ("Connect Outlook to invite guests"), with the editor's Save button (R39), which is the moment invitations go out; the event goes to Outlook with them as required attendees and Outlook sends the invitations, updates and cancellations (R26). If Outlook is down, the event is "Not sent to Outlook" and its guests are invited at the next sync. The editor's bin asks once ("Delete and tell the 2 guests?"). The coach never sets guests and never moves or deletes an event that has them (R22); it may add to-dos to it. The database checks the limit and the shape, refuses guests without a connection, on a series or a demo event, and refuses any change of an Outlook event's attendees. Not here: whether a guest accepted or declined, answering an invitation in the app, choosing guests from a list or a team, guests' names or pictures. A new guest can be added only to an event that has not ended ("Guests can only be invited to events that have not ended"): Outlook gets no past events (R26), so no invitation would ever go out; taking guests off a past event stays possible, and the editor says why the field is missing.

## 7 What is saved, where, and who can see it
- **Email, password:** Supabase Auth, the password only as a hash (it cannot be turned back into the password).
- **All rows of section 3:** Supabase (Postgres) with row-level security. Seen only by the owner.
- **Outlook access:** encrypted in `calendar_connection`, read only by our server; never in the browser or extension.
- **Card details:** Stripe only; we keep `stripe_customer_id` and `plan`.
- **AI chat:** messages and drafts saved; R22's data goes to the provider of Claude.
- **Tasks:** saved in the database, visible only to their user (R3).
- **To-dos inside an event:** saved in the database with their event, visible only to their user (R3); never sent to Outlook.
- **Guests of an event (R41):** e-mail addresses of other people, so personal data. Saved only in the owner's event (also an Outlook event's attendees and organizer), shown only to the owner (R3), sent only to Outlook (which invites them), never logged, never sent to Claude (R22).
- **Profile photo and display name** (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 28, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): personal data. In the database (the photo as bytes, at most 200,000), shown only to the user, their contacts and people the user invited (R43); the photo is served only from our own address, with headers that keep it from running as a page or being embedded by other sites. Never sent to Claude or Outlook, never logged. Deleted with the account.
- **Bio, LinkedIn link and time zone** (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 31, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): the bio and the link are personal data the user typed, in the database; shown, like the time zone from the settings, only to the user, their contacts and people the user invited (R43). Never sent to Claude or Outlook, never logged. Deleted with the account.
- **Contacts and invitations** (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 28, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): e-mail addresses of other users and addresses the user typed, so personal data. In the database with row-level security; a contact's address is seen by the two people only; a typed address only by its sender (R44). Never logged, never sent to Claude. Deleted with the account (the invitations someone sent to it stay in that sender's list as the address they typed).
- **Shares** (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 29, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): in the database with row-level security, seen by the owner and the viewer only, deleted with the contact or either account. What leaves the owner's rows, and only to that viewer, only while the share and the contact last: at `busy` the times of their events (merged), at `details` also titles and places; with the focus chart its weekly and daily totals. Computed at each request, never copied, never logged, never sent to Claude or Outlook. The viewer's browser remembers only which people's calendars to show (their user ids), never their events.
- **Shared lists** (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 30, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge) (withdrawn 15 Nov 2026 (spec 34, decided by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): not part of the app; the tables are kept empty and closed: no user can read or change the three tables any more, and no screen shows them; what follows describes them as they were): in the database with row-level security; a list, its members and its entries are seen only by its owner and members (R55), who see each other's display name and photo but no e-mail address of a non-contact. Never sent to Claude or Outlook, never in analytics, never logged. A list is deleted with its owner's account.
- **Demo contacts** (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 32, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): nothing about them is saved except the user's own choice (`demo_contacts`, seen only by the user); they are made up in the code and are no one's personal data. Never sent to Claude or Outlook.
- **Map in the event preview** (added 15 Nov 2026, spec 35, asked for by the owner; the team confirms with the merge): nothing is saved. To show the map, the browser loads OpenStreetMap's map with the place's coordinates, so OpenStreetMap learns those coordinates and the visitor's internet address, as with any map; no referrer is sent and the map runs sandboxed. This happens only after the user opened the preview for one of their own events with an address, never in advance. The address itself is looked up by our server (as for the travel time), never by the browser. Guests are matched to the user's own contacts on our server; no other address is looked up.
- **Extension:** Chrome's storage holds the block list and blocking state, on that computer only.

**Secrets:** Claude API key, Supabase service key and connection string, Microsoft client secret, the key that encrypts the Outlook access, the routing service key, Stripe secret and webhook keys, the e-mail sender's password. Only on the server as environment variables, never in the public repository, the browser or the extension. After a leak: revoke the key, create a new one, check the provider's usage.

**When a service is down** (no crash; calls give up after 10 seconds):

## 9 Open decisions
Each with its default.
