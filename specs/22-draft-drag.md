# Spec 22: Dragging the blocks of a draft in the chat
Asked for by the owner on 13 Nov 2026: "You give me the plan in the chat. It would be great if I could take the blocks there, as in the normal calendar, and move them back and forth, and only then confirm." Plan: `docs/ontology.md` (`draft_plan` and its `version`, `edit_draft`, `accept_draft`, R1, R4, R23, R38; changes of 13 Nov 2026 on this branch). Branch: `22-draft-drag`, built on `21-event-todos` (spec 21).

## Why
The coach's draft is close to what the person wants, but often an hour off here and there. Asking again costs a message (R21) and may change more than the one block. Dragging the block to the right time, as in the calendar, is quicker and leaves the rest of the draft alone.

## What
- [x] In the open draft only, the blocks the draft itself would create or move can be dragged: new events, and events the coach moves (dragging sets their proposed new time). Not draggable: the pale events already in the calendar, events the draft would delete, the old place of a moved event, the part of a block after midnight, and anything in a draft that is not open. Mouse and pen only, not touch (as in the calendar).
- [x] As in the calendar's day grid, with the same pure drag maths (`src/lib/events/drag.ts`): move a block up and down, drag its bottom edge to make it longer or shorter, snapped to 15 minutes, on its day; Escape cancels. To another day: by telling the coach. To-dos, title and goal stay with the block.
- [x] The open draft's day column shows the whole visible day (`day_start_hour` to `day_end_hour` of the settings, widened to everything it has to show), so there is room to drag. Drafts that are not open keep the short window around their blocks.
- [x] Each drop is the new action `edit_draft`: the draft, which block (a new event by its place in the list, or the event a move names), its new local start and end, and the draft's version the screen last saw (R4). Allowed while the draft is `open`. The server builds the draft as it would be and runs every check of R23 on it (`src/lib/chat/checks.ts`, the same module as for the assistant's answer and for accepting); the draft as it is must still pass too. One failed check refuses the change and nothing is saved (R1). Messages: "This would overlap another event" / "A new event lasts 15 minutes to 8 hours" / "An event can last at most 24 hours" / "End must be after start" / "This time is in the past" / "The coach plans only the next 14 days" / "A block stays on its day. Ask the coach to move it to another day" / "This draft is no longer open" / "Changed on another device. Reload to see the latest version" / "This draft is out of date. Ask again"; another user's draft, a missing one, a malformed id, a block the draft does not have and a delete: "Not found" (R3).
- [x] The block moves on the screen at once; when the server refuses, it goes back and the server's sentence shows in the card (`role="alert"`). While a change is being saved, no other block can be dragged and the buttons wait.
- [x] Nothing else changes: the calendar still changes only with "Add to calendar" / "Apply changes", which applies the draft as the person left it. A draft the person changed is marked `"draftNote": "adjusted by the person"` in the history sent to Claude, and the fixed prompt says to build on an adjusted draft and not to put its blocks back.
- [x] The list under each day column (for screen readers) always says the current times, also while a change is being saved.
- [x] Database (`supabase/migrations/20261126000000_draft_edits.sql`, additive): the column `app.draft_plans.version` (0 by default); a trigger that lets a draft's content change only while it is open and only one version at a time; the security definer function `app.chat_edit_draft`, which changes nothing but the start and end of one new event or one move of the user's own open draft, under a row lock, with the version check, and checks again what the database can (the block exists, end after start, 15 minutes to 8 hours for a new event, at most 24 hours for a move). The user's role gets no new right on the table. `main`, `19-coach`, `20-coach-changes` and `21-event-todos` work unchanged against it.

## Out of scope
Dragging with the keyboard (the calendar's drag has no keyboard form either; telling the coach, or the editor after applying, does the same); dragging to another day; dragging on touch screens; creating a new block by dragging on empty space in the draft; changing a block's title, goal or to-dos in the draft; undo of a single drag (the coach or another drag puts it back).
