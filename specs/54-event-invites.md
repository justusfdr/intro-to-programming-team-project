# Spec 54: Inviting contacts to an event, inside the app
> 19 Nov 2026, asked for by the owner (translated): "In the event editor it only says 'Connect Outlook to invite guests'. I must also be able to invite the people I have as contacts in the app." A port onto today's `main` (with PR #204) of work built and tested on 15 Nov 2026 but never merged: spec 36 (branch `37-event-invites`) in its last state on `origin/40-no-preview-photo-crop`, which includes spec 38 (no preview column: other people's events open a small card at the block) and commit b3eb6c0 (only blocks at the same time give up width). The database part, `supabase/migrations/20261210000000_event_invites.sql`, is already on `main` and in the live database. Ontology: `event_invitation`, `set_event_invites`, `answer_event_invite`, `read_invited_events`, R59 to R64, R3's exception (4), R22 and R26 confirmed, R41 distinguished, section 7, A36, A37, the out-of-scope list (each marked "added 19 Nov 2026, spec 54").

## Why
Guests (spec 24) are e-mail addresses that Outlook invites; without Outlook nobody can be invited, and nobody sees an answer. Since spec 53 people have real contacts in the app. Inviting them inside the app, with their answers, is the step the owner asked for; the database already has everything for it.

## What
- [x] **Inviting (R59).** The editor has "People in LockedIn" above the guests (whose heading is now "Guests by e-mail (Outlook)"): the user's real contacts, each with a box to tick, and for those already invited their answer as a mark and in words. It waits for Save like the other fields; Save then calls its own action (`POST /api/events/[id]/invites`), after the event's own fields. A new event from the editor is created first, then its ticked people are invited. Where R59 forbids it, the section says why ("Events of a series cannot invite people", "This event has ended", …) and lists who is invited; without contacts, a link to People. At most 20.
- [x] **What the invited person sees (R60).** The event in their calendar (day, week, month) from the moment they are invited, read only: title, time or days, place, who invited them. A click opens a small card at the block with title, date and time, place as text, "Invited by <name>", the other invited people with their marks, and Accept / Maybe / Decline with the current answer pressed.
- [x] **The look (decided 19 Nov 2026, no stripes).** No answer yet: white, dashed border in ink, the inviter's avatar and the title. Maybe: the same with lighter text and "Maybe". Accepted: solid like one of the viewer's own events without a goal, with the inviter's avatar. Declined: not in the grid, still in the list of invitations while the event has not ended. Shared calendars keep their colours (`src/lib/shares/colors.ts` unchanged).
- [x] **Answering (R61).** In the card and in the toolbar's "N invitations" list; saved at once, with a quiet line and a toast; the block changes at once. The sidebar's Calendar item has a badge with the number not answered.
- [x] **The owner's block.** A stack of up to 3 avatars with "+N"; its accessible name and its title list the people and their answers ("2 invited: Ben Brown accepted, Dora Dias no answer yet"). The editor's section shows the full list with marks.
- [x] **The same event twice.** When the inviter also shares their calendar and is shown, the shared block (timed or all-day) of that person with exactly the same start and end as a drawn invitation from them is left out (`withoutInvitedTwins`, `src/lib/shares/place.ts`). The source branch did nothing here.
- [x] **Width (b3eb6c0).** Only the viewer's blocks that run at the same time as a shared or invited block give up width for it; the rest of the day keeps the whole column. This also helps today's shared calendars.
- [x] **The coach (R63).** The owner's coach may not move or delete an event with invited people: the coach's calendar reader counts such an event as one with guests (`app.event_has_invites`), and who is invited is never read. Invited events are never sent to anyone's coach.

## Decisions
- **No database change.** The code calls the functions of `20261210000000_event_invites.sql` exactly as defined. No new dependency.
- **Ported, not copied.** `main` has moved a lot since 15 Nov; the invitation parts were re-applied by hand to `CalendarView`, `EventEditor`, `TimeGrid`, `Shared`, the sidebar and the coach.
- **Photos follow R43.** `app.may_see_profile` lets co-invited people see each other, but the photo route serves a photo only when `app.profile_card` gives a row. Co-invited people who are not the viewer's contacts are therefore shown by the name `app.invite_person_names` gives ("Invited person" without a display name) and their initials; the server never hands out a photo address for them.
- **Ticks instead of a drop-down.** The source had chips and a "Invite a contact…" list; the owner asked for "the people I have as contacts", so every contact is shown with a box.
- **Hover cards left out** (as in spec 53): the owner's block has a plain `title` and an accessible name; the editor lists everyone with marks.
- **The calendar page reads everything in its one transaction** (`loadCalendarPageWithInvites`); the existing loaders return exactly what they did.
- **Numbers.** The source's R59 to R64 and A36, A37 are the next free numbers on `main`; they stay. R3's exception is (4) here ((5) on the source branch).

## Out of scope
The coach finding a common time and inviting (spec 37); the demo invitation from Lea and demo contacts; hover cards; the preview column and its map (spec 35, withdrawn in spec 38); matching Outlook guests to contacts; the shared focus chart; lists. Also, as in spec 36: inviting people who are not contacts, proposing another time, comments, writing an accepted event to the invited person's Outlook, invitations to a series, invited events in the owner's month cells (no avatar stack there).
