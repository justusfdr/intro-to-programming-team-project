# Spec 21: To-dos inside an event
Asked for by the owner on 13 Nov 2026: "I want to say: 'Tomorrow I want to study from 12 to 1, and there I have the to-dos …'. Then the coach writes the to-dos down." Asked where they should go, he chose: inside the event (a list to tick off in the event's window, with a count like "2/3" on the block), not as tasks under a goal. Plan: `docs/ontology.md` (`event_todo`, `add_todo`, `rename_todo`, `tick_todo`, `move_todo`, `delete_todo`, `create_event`, `draft_plan`, `accept_draft`, `undo_draft`, R11, R22, R23, R38 to R40, A22; changes of 13 Nov 2026 on this branch). Branch: `21-event-todos`, built on `20-coach-changes` (spec 20). Open task B1.

## Why
An event often stands for a block of work with several small steps ("study 12 to 1: read chapter 3, exercises 1 to 4"). The owner wants those steps in the event itself, to tick off while it runs, and wants the coach to write them down when he says them.

## What
- [x] An event that is not from Outlook (timed or all-day) has up to 20 to-dos: a title of 1 to 100 characters, done or not, and a place in the list. A to-do belongs to exactly one event (not to a series) and is deleted with it. Outlook events have none: the editor shows no list, the server and the database refuse (R11 unchanged).
- [x] Actions, each saved at once (like "Mark done", not with the Save button of R39): `add_todo` (at the end), `rename_todo`, `tick_todo` (done or not done), `move_todo` (to another place: the prototype's drag and arrow keys; the others close up), `delete_todo`. Each answers with the event's whole list. Messages: "An event can have at most 20 to-dos" / "A to-do needs a title of 1 to 100 characters" / "To-dos cannot be added to Outlook events"; another user's event or to-do, a missing one and a malformed id: "Not found" (R3).
- [x] A change of a to-do does not change the event's `updated_at`, so ticking never makes the editor's Save look "changed on another device" (R4). To-dos are never sent to Outlook.
- [x] `create_event` takes an optional list (title and done): the editor of a new event ("Add details") holds a list and sends it with "Add event"; a series gets the list on every event; the calendar's undo of a delete brings the event back with its to-dos (`delete_event` answers with them, R38).
- [x] The editor shows the list right under WHEN, in the prototype's look (`components/calendar/SubtaskList.tsx`): the heading "To-dos" with "2/3", per row the grip (drag or ↑/↓), the box, the title (click to rename), the cross; then "Add a to-do". A block in the calendar shows "2/3" at the bottom right when it is tall enough (as in the prototype), and says it in its accessible name. The calendar's loaders count the to-dos in the same statement as the events.
- [x] The coach: a new event in a draft may carry 0 to 20 to-dos (each 1 to 100 characters after trimming), checked in `src/lib/chat/checks.ts`; one failed check rejects the whole answer. The answer format requires `todos` for each new event; the prompt says to put the things the person lists for an event into its to-dos, in their words, and never to invent any. The draft keeps them; the history sent to Claude carries a draft's to-dos ("add one more"); the calendar's own to-dos are never sent (R22). The draft card shows the count on the block and the titles under the day. Accepting creates the events with their to-dos in one transaction; undo removes them with their events, and an event the coach deleted comes back with its to-dos.
- [x] Database (`supabase/migrations/20261125000000_event_todos.sql`, additive): the table `app.event_todos` with a two-column link to the user's own event (deleted with it), row-level security, column rights (title, done and place; the event only when adding), checks (title 1 to 100 without spaces around it, place 0 or more, one to-do per place), a trigger with a per-event lock for the limit of 20 and the Outlook refusal. `app.chat_apply_draft` and `app.chat_undo_draft` are replaced by versions that also write to-dos (and keep everything else); the old `app.chat_accept_draft` refuses a draft with to-dos so the code of `19-coach` cannot accept one while dropping them. `main`, `19-coach` and `20-coach-changes` work unchanged against it.

## Out of scope
The coach adding to-dos to an event already in the calendar, or ticking them; notes inside an event (B2); to-dos in Outlook; a to-do's own due date or link to a task; dragging to-dos between events; undo of a single to-do change; the demo week with to-dos.
